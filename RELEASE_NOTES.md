# Glyph Studio 0.4.9

Prevail is now Glyph Studio. It is the same private AI harness for your life,
under a new name, and part of the Glyph family: Glyph Studio, Glyph Dictate,
Glyph Mobile, Glyph Record and Glyph Code. The engine inside it is Glyph Engine.

Nothing moves. Your vault, chats, settings, Keychain items and connections stay
where they are, and the `prevail` command still works. This update installs
over Prevail in place.

The app has a new icon too: the Glyph Studio mark in office green, in the
Dock, the menu bar and inside the app.

More at https://glyph.fru.dev/studio
