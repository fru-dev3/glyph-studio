# Wealth ideal state

## Purpose
Wealth is the foundation of freedom, not the goal.

## Trade-offs
- Peace of mind over the highest return
- Freedom over a bigger house
- Low fees over a familiar advisor

## What to avoid
- Never move money without my yes
- Never concentrate over 20% in one stock
- Never lifestyle creep

## Metrics you track
- Net worth, monthly
- Fee drag, yearly
- Savings rate
